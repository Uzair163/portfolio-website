About

Statistician at the Northern Ireland Civil Service Tech Lab, working across Vital Statistics, Education, Infrastructure and Climate.

I care about the last mile of analytics: the moment a model, a dashboard or a pipeline actually has to change what someone decides. That means as much attention on data quality, documentation and stakeholder sign-off as on the analysis itself.

My background sits at the intersection of engineering and statistics — a BSc in Electrical Engineering, an MSc in Business Analytics from Queen's University Belfast, and several years turning that into reproducible pipelines, forecasting models and Power BI dashboards for public sector and industry clients.

I'm a member of the Royal Statistical Society, hold AWS AI Practitioner and CompTIA Data+ certifications, and I'm currently working toward Microsoft's PL-300 and DP-900.

KTP Project

W&G Baird Analytics Pipeline

A time-boxed Knowledge Transfer Partnership project with W&G Baird, a Northern Ireland printing company, run through Queen's University Belfast. Built a full end-to-end analytics pipeline in R and Python, with a Power BI dashboard and a Streamlit companion app. Delivered customer concentration analysis, churn identification at two risk thresholds and a six-month revenue forecast, plus a board-ready presentation and recorded walkthrough.
